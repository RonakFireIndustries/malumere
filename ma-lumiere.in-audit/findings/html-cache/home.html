MEET YOUR PHYSICIAN

Dr. Rishita Ray

Founder & Aesthetic Physician, Ma Lumière

I believe good aesthetic care begins with understanding the skin — not simply choosing a procedure.

I am Dr. Rishita Ray, an Aesthetic Physician with a background in modern aesthetic medicine and a special interest in skin health, acne, acne scarring and pigmentation.

My approach brings together medical understanding, clinical assessment and aesthetic precision. From managing persistent skin concerns to planning advanced aesthetic treatments, I believe every face and every skin type deserves an individualised approach.

At Ma Lumière, I founded the clinic around a simple philosophy: diagnosis first, procedures second.

I believe in understanding the underlying concern, setting realistic expectations and recommending treatments based on individual needs — never procedures for the sake of procedures.

Whether the goal is healthier skin, improved texture, scar management or subtle facial rejuvenation, my focus is on thoughtful treatment planning, natural-looking outcomes and care that evolves with you.

Because good aesthetic medicine isn’t about changing who you are. It’s about helping you feel comfortable in your own skin.
