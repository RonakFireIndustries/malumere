Hair Loss & Hair Thinning

Clinical assessment and personalised plans for thinning, shedding and hair-fall concerns.

Hair loss and thinning can follow stress, hormones, nutrition, medication or genetics. At Ma Lumière we first understand the pattern and the cause, then map a plan suited to your scalp and goals.

Not every strand of hair fall is the same — some shed diffuse, some thin at the crown, some recede at the temples. A consultation helps identify what you are actually dealing with so the plan, not a generic product, does the work.

Every recommendation is made in-clinic after an examination; nothing here replaces a personal consultation.

How it works

Assessment of the scalp and hair history, followed by targeted treatments and a maintenance routine designed around the underlying pattern of loss.

Who it is for

Men and women noticing increased shedding, widening parts, reduced density or a family history of thinning.

Concerns it addresses

Diffuse hair fall, crown thinning, receding hairline, post-partum shedding, stress-related loss.

Treatment process

  1. Scalp and history assessment
  2. Identifying contributing factors
  3. Personalised in-clinic plan
  4. Home-care and maintenance routine

Expected results

Progress is gradual — hair cycles take months. Your dermatologist sets realistic milestones at the consultation rather than promising fixed timelines.

Preparation & aftercare

Before the treatment

Come with a note of recent illnesses, medications and any family history of thinning.

After the treatment

Follow the prescribed routine consistently; hair responds to sustained care, not one-off sessions.
